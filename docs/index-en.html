Yeoul

Turn your events into a timeline

Yeoul is a vertical timeline where the distance between events grows with the time that really passed. Three days and five days sit 3:5 apart, and a long stretch like two years folds into a dotted line labelled "2 years 4 months". A baby's first year, a trip, a project log — lay anything out just as time flowed.

📏 Time is distanceEvery gap is drawn at the same scale, so you can see at a glance where things were busy and where they were quiet.
📷 Find photos from that dayPick a date and Yeoul finds the photos you took that day in your library. If none fit, choose one yourself.
🏷️ Weave events together with tagsTap a tag to gather events scattered across your timelines into a new one, and save it if you like.
🎨 Four card designsClassic, Poster, Film or Margin: choose the look that suits each event.
☁️ iCloud syncYour records follow you across devices signed in with the same Apple Account, and are stored only in your own iCloud.
